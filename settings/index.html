Where Your Data Is Stored

Nawtch keeps everything — foods, logs, weight, programs, exercises, settings — locally in this browser, not on a server. Nothing leaves your device, but that also means clearing your browser's site data/cookies for this page, using private browsing, or uninstalling the browser can permanently erase your data. Export a backup below every so often to stay safe.
