Target/Day controls what counts toward your daily goals ▼
Target/Day must be greater than 0 for a food to count toward daily target calories, daily target cost, and weekly cost projections. A target of 0 means the food is still loggable but excluded from all targets.
Hiding a food removes it from tracking but keeps it saved ▼
Hide removes a food from the daily tracker, daily cost target, and expected weekly cost. The food stays in storage and can be restored any time with Show.
Barcode scanning a food/product is not always accurate ▼
The barcode scanner uses a community driven food library and may not always provide accurate nutritional information. Always double-check the details before logging a food item.
